Tips & tricks

Connect

  • Type channel names, press Enter or Connect
  • The red ✕ disconnects that platform
  • Your channels are saved — and live in the page URL

Expand

  • Expand (top right) fills the window with the chat
  • ✕ or Esc brings the page back
  • Bookmark or share the URL while expanded — it opens straight into it
  • Install the site as an app (browser address bar) — it opens with your last used channels

Watch the stream

  • When expanded, press ▶ (top right) — starts muted
  • Click the platform icons to switch source
  • YouTube appears once the channel is live

Alert sounds

  • Settings → Alert sounds: paste your alert overlay URL (StreamElements etc.) — its sound plays in the expanded chat
  • Click 🔊 Enable alert sound once per visit (browser rule)
  • Alert URLs stay in this browser — they are never part of the page link

OBS overlay

  • Click Copy OBS link above the chat feed, then paste it into the URL field of an OBS Browser Source
  • Transparent background — chat floats right on the stream image
  • Messages fade out after 60 s; &fade=120 changes the lifetime, &fade=0 never fades
  • Tune it: &size=20 (text px), &align=right, &max=10 (message count), &icons=0 (hide platform icons)

Good to know

  • Twitch badges and 7TV/BTTV/FFZ emotes show automatically — toggle them in Settings
  • Messages that @-mention a connected channel are highlighted
  • Filter chips hide platforms in your view only
  • Deleted messages stay, with a strikethrough
  • The feed keeps the latest 200 messages
